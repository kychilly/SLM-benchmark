### Problem 1:
The instructions on a 350-gram bag of coffee beans say that proper brewing of a large mug of pour-over coffee requires 20 grams of coffee beans. What is the greatest number of properly brewed large mugs of coffee that can be made from the coffee beans in that bag?

**Reasoning:** 
To determine how many large mugs of coffee can be brewed, we need to divide the total amount of coffee beans by the amount required for one mug.

**Final Answer:**
\[
\text{Number of mugs} = \frac{\text{Total grams of coffee}}{\text{Grams per mug}} = \frac{350}{20} = 17.5
\]
Since we can only brew whole mugs, the greatest number is \(17\).

### Problem 2:
Jerry wrote down the ones digit of each of the first 2025 positive squares: 1, 4, 9, 6, 5, 6, ... What is the sum of all the numbers Jerry wrote down?

**Reasoning:** 
The sequence of ones digits for squares repeats every 10 terms as follows: \(1, 4, 9, 6, 5, 6, 5, 6, 9, 6\). We need to find how many complete cycles of these 10 numbers are in the first 2025 positive squares and then sum them up.

**Final Answer:**
First, we determine the number of complete cycles:
\[
\text{Number of complete cycles} = \left\lfloor \frac{2025}{10} \right\rfloor = 202
\]
Each cycle sums to \(1 + 4 + 9 + 6 + 5 + 6 + 5 + 6 + 9 + 6 = 55\). So, the sum of these cycles is:
\[
202 \times 55 = 11110
\]
Next, we calculate the remaining terms. The remainder when dividing by 10 is \(2025 - 202 \times 10 = 25\). We need to sum the first 25 numbers in the sequence: \(1 + 4 + 9 + 6 + 5 + 6 + 5 + 6 + 9 + 6\).

Summing these:
\[
1 + 4 + 9 + 6 + 5 + 6 + 5 + 6 + 9 + 6 = 50
\]
Adding the sum of complete cycles and remaining terms:
\[
11110 + 50 = 11160
\]
